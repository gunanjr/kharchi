Kharchi Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Kharchi is an expense tracker and bill-splitting app that works entirely on your phone. We do not run servers that receive your data, and the app has no account system.

Summary

  • Your transactions, budgets, groups and notes are stored only on your device.
  • The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and no third-party SDK that collects data.
  • The Google Play version of the app does not request internet permission, so it cannot send data off your phone.
  • We (the developer) cannot see your data.

What the app stores on your device

Amounts, merchants, categories, notes, dates, account names and last four digits you provide, budgets, group and friend names you type, UPI IDs you choose to save for friends, settings, and the history of questions you ask the built-in assistant. A one-way hash of each message you paste or share is kept only to prevent duplicate entries. The text of a message you paste or share is not stored after the amount, merchant and date have been read from it.

Messages you paste or share

The app does not read your SMS inbox and does not run in the background to monitor messages. When you choose to paste or share a bank message into the app, it is analysed on your phone only. Nothing is uploaded.

Permissions the app may request

  • Notifications (optional): to show reminders that you set yourself, such as asking a friend to settle up.
  • Biometrics / screen lock (optional): to protect the app with your fingerprint, face or screen lock.
  • Vibration: for button feedback.
  • Run at start-up: to restore reminders you set after the phone restarts.

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files or SMS. Importing or exporting a file uses Android’s own file picker, and you choose exactly which file.

Sharing and exporting

Nothing is shared unless you do it yourself, for example by exporting a CSV or backup file, sharing a group summary, or opening your UPI app to pay a friend. Files and messages you send to others are governed by the app you send them with.

Your control

You can export all your data, import it again, or erase everything with Settings → Export & backup → Delete all my data. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your phone. Android’s automatic cloud backup is disabled for this app, so your financial data is not copied to Google Drive.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If a future version adds an optional online feature such as syncing or a paid subscription, this policy will be updated before it is released and the feature will be clearly explained and opt-in. A paid subscription would be processed by Google Play; we would receive only the purchase status, never your financial records.
