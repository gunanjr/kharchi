Privacy Policy

App: Kharchi (expense tracker and bill splitter)   Last updated: REPLACE_DATE

Kharchi works on your phone. We do not operate servers that receive your data.

What the app accesses

With your permission, the app receives bank and UPI transaction text messages (SMS) solely to create expense entries and ask you what each was for. Messages that are not bank or UPI transactions, such as one-time passwords, promotions and personal chats, are discarded immediately on your device and are never stored, logged or shown.

What is stored

On your device only: amount, merchant, account last four digits, date, your categories and notes, your groups and friends (names you type), and a one-way hash of each processed message used to prevent duplicates. The text of the message is deleted after it has been understood.

What is shared

Nothing. Messages and transactions are never uploaded, sold or shared with anyone. The app contains no advertising and no analytics.

Permissions

  • Receive SMS: to detect bank/UPI transaction alerts (optional; the app works without it).
  • Notifications: to ask “What was this for?” and show reminders.
  • Biometrics: optional app lock.

Your choices

You can revoke SMS access in Android Settings at any time, limit which sender IDs are read inside the app, export your data, or erase everything with Settings → Export & backup → Delete all my data. Uninstalling the app removes all data.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If a future version adds optional online features such as syncing, this policy will be updated before release and those features will be opt-in.
