BruhMe Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

BruhMe is an expense tracker and bill-splitting app for Android. It works on your phone. There is no BruhMe account, and we do not run servers that receive your entries.

Summary

  • Your entries, budgets, goals, groups, notes and photos are stored on your phone.
  • BruhMe does not send your entries to us. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools.
  • Nothing leaves the app unless you choose to export, share, back up or send it.

What the app stores on your phone

Amounts, merchants, categories, tags, notes, dates, account names and the last four digits you type, budgets, goals, group and friend names you type, UPI IDs you choose to save (your own and your friends'), photos you attach (bills, receipts, icons you pick for a merchant), settings, and the questions you tap in the built-in assistant. When you paste or share a bank message, it is read on your phone; only the amount, merchant and date you confirm are kept, plus a one-way fingerprint to avoid duplicates. The message text is not stored.

Photos

Taking a photo opens your phone's own camera app; choosing one uses Android's photo picker. You decide exactly which photo the app receives. Photos are kept in the app's private storage and are never uploaded by BruhMe. Bills you scan are read on your phone.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): reminders you set and, if you allow them, a weekly summary.
  • Fingerprint, face or screen lock (optional): to lock the app.
  • Vibration: small feedback when you tap or save.
  • Run at start-up: to restore reminders after the phone restarts.

BruhMe does not ask for your contacts, location, microphone, SMS or call log, and does not read your messages in the background.

Backups and BruhMe Vault

You can save a backup file yourself from Export & backup and send it wherever you like. With BruhMe Vault (part of BruhMe Pro), the app saves an encrypted backup to a folder you pick with Android's folder picker, for example in Google Drive. The file is stored by the service you chose, not by us. It is encrypted with a key that only your recovery code can open; we never see the code and cannot recover it for you. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for this app.

Purchases

BruhMe Pro is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. BruhMe receives only the purchase status needed to turn Pro on; we do not receive your card or bank details.

Sharing

When you share a summary, a pay card or a message, export a file, or open a UPI app to pay or get paid, it goes only where you send it, under that app's terms. BruhMe never sends money and never marks anything as paid on its own.

Your control

Export all your data, restore a backup, or erase everything in More, Export & backup, Delete all my data. Uninstalling the app removes its data from your phone. Backup files you saved elsewhere are not affected.

Children

BruhMe is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date before the change takes effect.
