# Store listing (English, India)

**App name (30):** Kharchi: Expense & Split
**Short description (80):** Track every rupee and split bills with friends. Private, no ads, no limits.

**Full description:**
Kharchi is the expense tracker that does the boring part for you, and the bill splitter that never asks you to upgrade.

**Know where your money goes**
• Bank and UPI texts become expenses automatically (on your phone only), then Kharchi asks one question: "What was this for?" Reply right from the notification.
• Add an expense in about 3 seconds from a Quick Settings tile, a home-screen widget or a launcher shortcut.
• Budgets with gentle alerts, category donut, monthly trends, daily heatmap, subscription finder and spending forecasts.
• Ask the built-in assistant: "How much on food last month?", "Can I save this month?", "Who do I owe?". It runs offline.

**Split with anyone, free**
• Unlimited groups, friends and expenses. No daily limits, no paywall.
• Equal, exact, percentage, shares, adjustment and itemised splits; multiple payers; multi-currency.
• Smart "simplify debts" finds the fewest payments to settle up. Pay by UPI in one tap.
• Your share of every group bill appears in your personal tracker automatically.

**Private by design**
• Everything is stored on your device. No account needed.
• Messages are read on-device and never uploaded; the text is deleted after parsing.
• App lock with fingerprint/face. Export or delete all your data anytime.
